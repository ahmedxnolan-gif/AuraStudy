## 🚀 What is AuraStudy?

**AuraStudy** is a next-generation, cyberpunk-themed AI-powered study companion designed to transform how you learn. Whether you are prepping for exams, breaking down dense technical documentation, or looking to test your knowledge with high-difficulty adaptive quizzes, AuraStudy handles it all instantly in a stunning **Glassmorphism UI**.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* **📄 Advanced Document Parser:** Built-in client-side processing using `PDF.js` and `Mammoth` to seamlessly extract text from PDFs, DOCX, and TXT files.
* **🧠 Deep Pedagogical Explanations:** Goes beyond lazy bullet points. It breaks down concepts step-by-step from scratch, complete with real-world analogies, key terms, and common mistakes to avoid.
* **🌍 Multi-Tone & Multi-Language Support:** Instant explanations in **Egyptian Arabic (عامية مصرية 🇪🇬)**, **Simplified MSA**, or **Clear English**.
* **⚔️ 4-Tier Adaptive Quiz Engine:** Test your mastery across four rigorous difficulty levels:
  * 🟢 **Easy:** Direct concept checks.
  * 🟡 **Medium:** Comprehensive understanding.
  * 🔵 **Hard:** Deep analytical thinking.
  * 🔥 **Legendary:** Extreme critical thinking and complex edge-case scenarios!
* **💬 Interactive AI Tutor Chat:** Ask follow-up questions, request real-life examples, generate flashcards, or simulate exam conditions with your personal tutor.
* **🎨 Dynamic Cyberpunk Themes:** Switch between 10+ custom atmospheric neon themes (Cyber, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Midnight, and more).
* **🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy:** Your Gemini API key and data stay strictly in your browser's local storage. Zero server tracking.

## 🔑 Quick Start Guide

1. **Get a Free API Key:** Head over to [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) and generate your free Gemini API key in seconds.
2. **Launch & Setup:** Open the [Live Demo](https://ahmedxnolan-gif.github.io/AuraStudy/), expand the *Gemini AI Settings*, and paste your API key.
3. **Upload & Conquer:** Drop your study material (PDF/Text), choose your preferred explanation depth and language, and hit **Explain Simply & Thoroughly**!

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind-inspired custom CSS.
* **UI & Aesthetics:** Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties, Google Fonts (*Space Grotesk* & *Cairo*).
* **Rendering & Math:** MathJax for formal equations, Custom Markdown parsing engine.
* **AI Core:** Google Gemini API (`gemini-3.8-flash`).
