## 🎯 // WHAT IS AURA STUDY?
**AuraStudy by NOLAN** is a high-performance, dark-neon academic web application engineered to completely revolutionize how students dissect and master study materials. 

Instead of reading heavy textbooks or messy study sheets page by page, you simply drop your files—whether it's a dense **PDF, DOCX, or raw text notes**—and watch the AI engine act as an expert personal tutor. It breaks down complex concepts, formulas, and chapters into crystal-clear, deep explanations at lightning speed, complete with interactive practice quizzes and a built-in AI chat assistant.
